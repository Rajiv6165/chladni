# Chladni Demo Recording Script

**Target Length:** 45 - 60 seconds

## Setup (0:00 - 0:05)
1. Open the live link in a fresh browser window.
2. Click **SYSTEM INITIATE**.
3. Allow camera permissions if prompted.
4. Briefly hover over the **HOW TO PLAY** overlay so it's visible, then click **ACKNOWLEDGE** to dismiss it.

## The Pitch & Single Hand (0:05 - 0:20)
1. Bring your left hand into the frame.
2. Pinch index and thumb together to trigger a note (C3, low pitch).
3. Slowly move your pinched hand upwards in the Y-axis to sweep the pitch up to C5.
4. *Visual cue:* Point out how the Chladni patterns in the background snap to different integer modes as the pitch passes through harmonic frequencies. The "sand" will dance and settle on nodal lines.

## Adding the Right Hand (0:20 - 0:40)
1. While still holding the left-hand pinch (sustaining a note), bring your right hand into the frame.
2. Move your right hand **Left to Right (X-axis)** to gradually increase the Reverb. The sound should become vast and echoing.
3. Move your right hand **Up and Down (Y-axis)** to sweep the Low-Pass Filter cutoff. Pull it down to muffle the sound, push it up to open it back up.
4. Release the left-hand pinch to show the delay tails ringing out.

## The Fallback (0:40 - 0:55)
1. Close the browser tab.
2. Open a new tab, but this time **block** camera permissions in the browser address bar before clicking start, OR physically cover your webcam.
3. Click **SYSTEM INITIATE**.
4. The UI will show `OPTICAL SENSOR OFFLINE. MANUAL OVERRIDE ENGAGED.`
5. Use your mouse/trackpad to click and drag the rotary dials (Cutoff, Resonance, Reverb) to demonstrate the fallback mouse controls.

## End (0:55 - 1:00)
1. Stop recording.
